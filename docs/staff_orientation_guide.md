## Staff Operations & Daily CMS Quick-Reference Guide

> **Station Masthead:** Nyanthepa Community Radio, Nsanje Boma, Malawi  
> **Frequency:** 107.6 MHz FM  
> **Staff Portal URL:** `https://nyanthepa.mw/admin` (or `http://localhost:3000/admin` in local environment)

### Introduction for Station Staff

Welcome to the **Nyanthepa Content Management System (CMS)**. This system allows you to update news stories, the weekly broadcast schedule, podcast archives, and sports league tables across the official website and mobile app without touching any code.

---

### 1. Publishing a News Story or Flood Alert
1. In the staff sidebar, click **News & Bulletins**.
2. Click the **Write New Story** button at the top right.
3. Fill in:
   - **Headline:** Clear broadcast title (e.g. *Bangula Cotton Farmers Welcome New Market Weighing Stations*).
   - **Category:** Select *Agriculture*, *Flood Preparedness*, *Health*, *Sports*, or *Culture*.
   - **Summary (Standfirst):** 1–2 sentences shown in card previews.
   - **Full Article:** The complete report.
   - **Flags:**
     - Check **Feature on Homepage Banner** to place it at the very top.
     - Check **Broadcast Emergency Push Alert** if this is a flood or critical weather warning.
4. Click **Publish Story**. The story goes live immediately on both website and mobile app.

---

### 2. Updating the Broadcast Schedule Timetable
1. In the sidebar, click **Broadcast Schedule**.
2. Click the day tab you wish to update (e.g. *Monday* or *Saturday*).
3. Click **Add Show Slot** or click the **Edit** icon next to an existing show.
4. Enter:
   - **Start & End Time:** In 24-hour format (e.g. `06:00` and `09:00`).
   - **Program Title:** Show name in Chisena and/or English.
   - **Anchor Names:** On-air presenter(s).
   - **Language:** *Chisena*, *Chisena / EN*, or *English*.
5. Click **Save Show**. The monospace timetable updates across all platforms in real time.

---

### 3. Uploading Audio Podcasts & Cultural Recordings
1. In the sidebar, click **Podcasts & Audio**.
2. Click **Upload Audio / Podcast**.
3. Provide the direct **MP3 Audio URL** and optional **YouTube Video Link**.
4. Confirm the **Rights-Cleared** checkbox to ensure compliance with the Copyright Act of Malawi.
5. Click **Publish Audio**. Listeners can stream in-browser or download for offline listening.

---

### 4. Updating Football Log Tables
1. In the sidebar, click **Sports Log Tables**.
2. Select the target tab: **Nsanje District League**, **FDH Bank Cup**, or **English Premier League**.
3. Edit the numbers directly in the table (*Played*, *Won*, *Drawn*, *Lost*, *Points*).
4. Click **Save & Publish Standings**. The table reorders by points and goal difference automatically.

---

### 5. Managing Complaints & Regulatory Inquiries
1. In the sidebar, click **Feedback Inbox**.
2. Submissions arrive chronologically (newest first).
3. If a submission is marked **Urgent** or categorized as an **Editorial Complaint**:
   - Review the details with the Station Manager.
   - Update the status to **Reviewed** or **Resolved**.
   - If unresolved within statutory timelines, note the complaint for **MACRA** or **Media Council of Malawi** liaison.
